A Listening Ear

Feeling lost about your studies, having a low day, or just wanting someone to talk to—these are all reasons to reach out to your mentor. Mentors are here to listen and are ready to help you think things through.

Nonetheless, if you are experiencing ongoing psychological pressure, you may contact the University Counselling Centre, which can provide more specialised support. We can also assist with the referral.
