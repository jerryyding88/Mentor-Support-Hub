BNBU MCP Programme

BNBU Mentor Caring Programme (MCP) brings together 4 roles, to support you throughout university life:

  • Academic Advisor: Focuses on the transition, academic planning and scholastic guidance to help students adapt to university life and achieve academic success, targeted at freshman and sophomore students.
  • Mentor: Provides daily management and holistic support, caring for your well-being, everyday concerns, and general assistance, targeted at all year BNBU students.
  • Faculty Coordinator of Student Affairs: Serves as the faculty-level coordinator and primary contact for complex cases.
  • Peer Mentor: Outstanding senior students who help first-year students adapt to campus life.

What is a Mentor?

Mentors are tasked with the quotidian management and holistic support of students, covering all BNBU freshman to senior students. They provide support encompassing daily well-being related issues, quotidian guidance, and general assistance.

What can we help?

  • General Academic Guidance
  • A Listening Ear
  • Future Pathway Exploration and Information Sharing
  • Daily assistance
